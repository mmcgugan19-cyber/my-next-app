### Info tooltips for supplementary details
- Use an "i" icon (info circle) to hide secondary/explanatory content behind a hover tooltip
- Keeps the UI clean — only show essential info by default, details on demand
- Good for legal context, definitions, "why this matters" explanations

### Pre-drafted emails for action steps
- Generate ready-to-send email drafts for steps that require contacting institutions (banks, SSA, insurers, etc.)
- User reviews, personalizes, and sends — reduces friction on intimidating tasks
- Could integrate with email client or just copy-to-clipboard

### Paywall gating on process steps
- Blur/lock later steps of the guided process behind a paywall
- Which steps get gated TBD — will designate as we build out the flow
- UX: show blurred preview so users can see value before paying
